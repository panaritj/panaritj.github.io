About me

Hi, my name is Panarit Jahiri. I have been working with technology in one way or another since I was a kid. For some years, I worked in fully-algorithmic and automated trading of flexible energy systems in electricity markets. Currently, I am based in Berlin, working as an engineer in the AI Core unit at SAP, focusing on Generative AI.

This is where I document my thoughts on engineering, “philosophy”, complex concepts, and building software. I am all about optimizing my computer workflow.

I also do quite a bit of photography on the side. A bit of everything: landscape, street, abstract. You can find my visual work on Instagram.

Opinions are my own.
